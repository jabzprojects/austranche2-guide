AML/CTF Compliance Guide for Australian Lawyers and Conveyancers

Tranche 2 obligations under the AML/CTF Act now apply to lawyers and conveyancers who provide designated services. Use this guide to check whether your practice is captured, what you need to do, what you must keep doing and which forms to use. Select a topic below to begin.
